## Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

### PTO Accrual

All full-time employees accrue PTO based on their tenure with the company.
PTO accrues on a bi-weekly basis (every pay period) starting from the first day
of employment:

| Years of Service | Annual PTO   | Bi-weekly Accrual |
|------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| 0–1 years        | 15 days      | 0.577 days        |
| 1–3 years        | 20 days      | 0.769 days        |
| 3–5 years        | 25 days      | 0.962 days        |
| 5+ years         | 30 days      | 1.154 days        |

Part-time employees (20–29 hours/week) accrue at 50% of the full-time rate.

### PTO Usage and Approval

To request PTO, employees must:
1. Submit a PTO request in BambooHR at least 5 business days in advance for
   requests of 3 or more days. Requests of 1–2 days require 2 business days notice.
2. Notify your direct manager via Slack or email on the same day as submitting
   the BambooHR request.
3. For PTO during company-wide critical periods (end of quarter, annual reviews,
   product launches), requests require manager AND department head approval.

### PTO Carryover and Payout

- **Carryover limit**: Up to 5 PTO days may be carried over to the following
  calendar year. Any unused PTO above 5 days is forfeited on January 1.
- **Payout on termination**: Unused accrued PTO is paid out at your current
  hourly rate upon termination (voluntary or involuntary), as required by law.
- **No negative PTO**: PTO cannot be used before it is accrued. Employees who
  have exhausted their PTO balance must take unpaid leave or use sick days.

### Sick Days

Sick days are separate from PTO and provided as follows:
- All employees receive 10 sick days per calendar year, provided upfront on
  January 1 (non-accruing). New hires receive a prorated amount.
- Sick days do not carry over to the following year and are not paid out
  on termination.
- A doctor's note is required for sick absences of 4 or more consecutive days.

## Remote Work Policy

### Eligibility and Hybrid Expectations

All engineering, product, and data roles are eligible for full remote work.
Sales, customer success, and operations roles require a minimum of 3 days
per week in the office (if the employee is within 50 miles of an office location).

### Home Office Setup

The company provides a one-time home office stipend of $1,500 for equipment
(desk, chair, monitor, peripherals). Eligible employees can submit receipts
via Expensify within 90 days of their hire date or after a role change to a
remote-eligible position.

**What the stipend covers:** Desk, chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset,
webcam, lighting equipment, and ergonomic accessories.

**What it does not cover:** Desktop computers, laptops (provided separately by IT),
internet costs (see below), or personal devices.

### Internet Reimbursement

Remote employees receive a monthly internet stipend of $75, paid as a taxable
benefit via payroll. No receipts required — the stipend is added to your monthly
paycheck automatically. To enroll, submit a Remote Work Agreement form to HR
within 30 days of starting remote work.

### Coworking Spaces

Remote employees may expense up to $300/month for coworking space memberships
via Expensify. The coworking space must be a recognized provider (WeWork, Regus,
Industrious, or equivalent). Submit receipts with the tag "Remote-Coworking".

### Time Zone Policy

Remote employees must be available for synchronous collaboration during
**10:00 AM – 3:00 PM Eastern Time** on weekdays. Outside these hours, async
communication is expected. Engineers in significantly different time zones (>6h
from ET) must discuss overlapping hours with their manager before acceptance.

## Performance Review Cycle

### Review Schedule

Performance reviews occur twice per year:
- **Mid-year review**: Conducted in July (for the Jan–Jun period)
- **Annual review**: Conducted in January (for the Jul–Dec period)

**The annual review determines:**
- Merit-based salary adjustments (effective March 1)
- Promotion decisions (effective March 1)
- Equity refresh grants (vesting begins April 1)

**The mid-year review determines:**
- Performance improvement plans (PIPs) for underperforming employees
- Spot bonuses for exceptional contributors
- Role level adjustments (rare, requires VP approval)

### Review Process

1. **Self-assessment** (2 weeks before review): Employees complete a self-review
   in Lattice covering: key accomplishments, areas for growth, and goals for the
   next period.

2. **Peer feedback** (1 week before review): Each employee nominates 3–5 peers.
   Managers select 2 from the list and add 1 mandatory. Feedback is anonymous.

3. **Manager assessment**: Managers complete a written assessment covering:
   performance vs. expectations, collaboration, technical skills (for ICs),
   or leadership effectiveness (for managers).

4. **Calibration session**: Department heads calibrate ratings across teams to
   ensure consistency in how "meets expectations" and "exceeds expectations"
   are applied across the organization.

5. **Review meeting**: Managers meet 1:1 with employees to discuss the review.
   Employees receive their rating before the meeting to allow time to process.

### Performance Ratings

| Rating             | Description                                                |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| Exceptional        | Significantly above expectations; top 10% of peers        |
| Exceeds Expectations | Consistently above expectations; top 25% of peers       |
| Meets Expectations | Performs as expected for level; solid contributor          |
| Needs Improvement  | Below expectations in at least one key area               |
| Unsatisfactory     | Significantly below expectations; PIP required            |

### Merit Increase Guidelines

Salary increases are based on the performance rating and current pay positioning
within the salary band for the role:

| Rating             | Below Band Midpoint | At Midpoint | Above Midpoint |
|--------------------|--------------------|-------------|----------------|
| Exceptional        | 8–12%              | 6–9%        | 4–6%           |
| Exceeds Expectations | 5–8%             | 4–6%        | 3–5%           |
| Meets Expectations | 3–5%               | 2–4%        | 0–3%           |
| Needs Improvement  | 0%                 | 0%          | 0%             |

## Benefits Overview

### Health Insurance

The company offers three health plan tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold) through Aetna.
The company covers 80% of the employee premium and 50% of dependent premiums.
Annual open enrollment is in November; changes take effect January 1.

- **Deductibles**: Bronze $2,000 / Silver $1,000 / Gold $500 (individual)
- **Out-of-pocket max**: Bronze $6,000 / Silver $4,000 / Gold $2,000 (individual)
- **Copay (primary care)**: $40 / $30 / $20
- **Copay (specialist)**: $60 / $50 / $40

### Dental and Vision

- **Dental** (Guardian): 100% preventive, 80% basic, 50% major. $1,500 annual max.
- **Vision** (VSP): $200 frame allowance, $150 contact lens allowance per year.

### 401(k) Retirement Plan

- The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of salary.
- Vesting: 100% immediate for both employee and company contributions.
- Plan provider: Fidelity. Enrollment is automatic on day 1 at 3% contribution
  rate (opt out or change in Fidelity portal within 30 days to avoid auto-enrollment).

### Learning and Development

- **Annual L&D budget**: $2,000 per employee per year for courses, conferences,
  books, and certifications. Reimbursed via Expensify with tag "L&D".
- **Conference attendance**: Employees may attend one major industry conference
  per year. Travel and accommodation covered separately from the L&D budget.
- **Internal learning**: Weekly "Engineering Lightning Talks" on Fridays at noon ET.
  Monthly "Book Club" — books are expensed under L&D budget.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to uphold the company's values of Transparency,
Respect, and Excellence. The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

- Discrimination or harassment based on protected characteristics (race, gender,
  age, religion, national origin, disability, sexual orientation, pregnancy, etc.)
- Retaliation against employees who report violations in good faith
- Sharing confidential company information with unauthorized parties
- Use of company resources for personal financial gain
- Workplace violence or threats of violence

**Reporting**: All violations should be reported to HR (hr@yourcompany.com) or
via the anonymous reporting hotline: 1-800-XXX-XXXX. Reports can also be made
directly to the People team in person.

**Investigation**: HR investigates all reports within 5 business days. The
complainant is notified of the outcome within 30 days. Substantiated violations
result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
